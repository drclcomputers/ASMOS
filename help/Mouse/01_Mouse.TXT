# Mouse

Mouse sets up the mouse, on the PS/2 port or a serial port (COM). It
opens from Settings > Devices: double-click the mouse.

- Pointer speed: how far the pointer goes when the mouse moves. Slow
  helps with a jumpy mouse or exact drawing, fast with a big screen.
- Double-click speed: how quick the two clicks of a double-click must
  be. Fast wants them close together; slow lets them be further apart.
  Try it on the box under it: a double-click turns it green.
- Swap the left and right buttons: for the left hand. From the next
  click, the right button is the one that clicks.
- Every change works at once. OK keeps them (in ASMOS.CFG); Cancel, Esc
  or the window's X put back the old ones. Defaults: the normal speeds,
  the buttons as they were made.
