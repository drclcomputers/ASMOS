# Joystick

Joystick sets up a stick on the game port (the 15-pin port of a sound
card). It opens from Settings > Devices: double-click the joystick.

- The window shows the stick live: a dot for each pair of axes, the dead
  zone as a blue frame, and the buttons.
- Calibrate takes two steps. First let go of the stick and press Next (or
  a button on the stick). Then roll it to every edge a few times and press
  Next. Without it the stick is taken as centred when ASMOS started.
- Dead zone: how much of the middle counts as centred. A worn stick that
  drifts needs more.
- Reverse turns an axis around (X, Y, then Z and R of a second stick).
- Reset goes back to no calibration. Detect looks for the stick again; the
  game port can't tell when one is plugged in.
- OK keeps the settings in ASMOS.CFG; Cancel puts back the old ones.

Programs read the calibrated values: -100 to 100 on each axis. In the
shell, joy shows them. QEMU and Bochs have no game port; in 86Box add a
joystick in Settings > Input devices.
