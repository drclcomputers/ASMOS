# Files and disks

Every disk is a folder at the top: /HDA and /HDB (hard disks), /FDD0 and
/FDD1 (floppy drives), /CD0 (the CD drive), /NET0 (a server). FileF shows
them all under Drives.

- New names may have letters, digits, the dot and - _ # $ % & + = @ ! ~
  ( ) [ ] { }. No spaces: use _ or - instead. Names already on a disk
  keep what they have.
- Long names are up to 63 characters. DOS sees a short 8.3 name too.
- Deleting puts things in the Trash. Empty the Trash to free the space.

# Floppies

A floppy shows on the desktop once it has been read. To read one you
put in, open its drive in FileF's Drives. Taking a disk out is noticed by
itself. Use Eject before taking a disk out, so what is waiting gets
written first.

# CDs

A CD shows on the desktop by itself, with its name. Apps on it appear in
Apps > CDROM while it is in. An audio CD shows its tracks as
Track01.cda... and Audio Player plays them. Right-click the CD to eject
it.

# Servers

FileF's Drive > Connect to Server makes a folder on an FTP server the
drive NET0, with an icon on the desktop. Copy to it and from it as with
any disk. Right-click its icon to disconnect. In the shell: ftp mount and
ftp umount.

ftp startsrv in the shell lets other computers in: they see the drives
as folders. ftp stopsrv ends it. Anyone on the network can come in, so
use -p and a password, or -r to let them only read.
