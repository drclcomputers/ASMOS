# Picture Viewer

Picture Viewer shows pictures: BMP (Windows and OS/2, 1 to 32 bits a
pixel, RLE-packed too), GIF, JPEG and ASMDraw's .PIC. Double-click one
in FileF, or File > Open.

- A picture bigger than the window scrolls: the bars, the arrows, PgUp
  and PgDn (Ctrl+PgUp/PgDn sideways), Home and End (Ctrl: the corners),
  or drag the picture with the mouse.
- On a 256-colour screen (Settings > Display) it is shown in 256
  colours; on a 16-colour one in the 16. Each colour becomes the
  nearest the screen has.
- View > Dithering mixes dots of the nearest colours, for photos. Turn it
  off for drawings with flat colours.
- Edit > Copy (Ctrl+C) puts the picture on the clipboard, in the
  colours it is shown in: paste it in ASMDraw. Up to 320 KB (a 640 x 480
  picture is 300 KB).
- A big BMP fills in while it is read. A GIF or JPEG is read whole
  (files up to 2 MB); one too big for the memory is shown smaller, and
  the status line says so. With little memory free (other apps open, a
  small machine), pictures are shown smaller sooner.
- Not shown: PNG, and progressive JPEGs (some photos from the web).
