# ClipView

ClipView is the scrapbook: the last 20 things copied, text and
pictures, the newest at the top. The one on the clipboard now is
marked with >.

- Pick one to see it below: the text in full, or the picture (made
  smaller if it does not fit).
- Use (or double-click it) puts it back on the clipboard, to paste
  again.
- Delete takes it out of the scrapbook; Clear all empties it.
- Save... keeps it in a file: text as a .TXT, a picture as a .BMP.
- Pictures come from ASMDraw's selection and Picture Viewer's Edit > Copy.
  Paste one in ASMDraw, or in a folder (FileF, the desktop) to make a
  PICTURE.BMP there.
- Big pictures fill it fast: when they do, the oldest picture goes
  first.
