# Audio CDs

Put an audio CD in the drive and press CD (or C): its tracks replace
the CD tracks in the list, and the first one plays.

- The drive plays the music itself and sends it down its audio cable to
  the sound card's CD input. No sound? Check that cable, or use the
  headphone socket on the drive.
- The volume slider turns the sound card's CD input up and down.
- A track's file (Track01.cda) can be copied: it plays while its CD is
  in the drive.
