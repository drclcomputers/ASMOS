# Playing sounds

Audio Player plays WAV files on the Sound Blaster, MIDI files on the FM
synth, and audio CDs from the CD drive.

- Open puts a file in the list and plays it; Add only puts it in the
  list. Double-click a song in the list to play it.
- Space plays and pauses, S stops, R goes back to the start, N plays the
  next one, L repeats this one.
- Click the bar to jump there (WAV and CDs).
- + and - change the volume.

The list and the volume are kept for next time.
