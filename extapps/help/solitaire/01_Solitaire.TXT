# Solitaire

Three games in one: Klondike, Spider and FreeCell. Game > Game Type picks
one; the next deal is of that kind.

- Drag a card, or a run of them, to where it goes. A click sends a card
  where it fits best: up to a suit's pile first, else onto a column.
- Click the stock (or press Space) to turn cards in Klondike, or to deal
  a card onto every column in Spider.
- Ctrl+Z takes back a move, H shows a move worth making, F2 deals again.

# Klondike

Build the four suits up from Ace to King on the top right. The columns go
down in alternate colours, red on black; only a King goes into an empty
column. Draw 1 turns one card at a time, Draw 3 three.

# Spider

Two decks, in one, two or four suits. Any card goes on one a rank higher,
but only a run of one suit moves together. A whole suit, King down to Ace,
leaves the table; eight of them win. Every column needs a card before the
stock deals.

# FreeCell

All cards are face up. Build the suits up on the right, the columns down
in alternate colours. Each of the four cells on the left holds one card.
Several cards move at once when there are free cells or empty columns to
do it through. Almost every deal can be won.

Deals are numbered: Game > Select Game plays one again (FreeCell's are the
classic ones). Statistics counts the games and the best times. A game in
progress is kept for next time.
