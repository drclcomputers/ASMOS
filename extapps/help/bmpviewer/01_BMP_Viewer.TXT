# BMP Viewer

BMP Viewer shows .BMP pictures: Windows and OS/2 ones, 1, 4, 8, 16, 24
and 32 bits a pixel, RLE-packed ones too. Double-click a .BMP in FileF,
or File > Open.

- A picture bigger than the window scrolls: the bars, the arrows, PgUp
  and PgDn (Ctrl+PgUp/PgDn sideways), Home and End (Ctrl: the corners),
  or drag the picture with the mouse.
- On a 256-colour screen (Settings > Display) it is shown in 256
  colours; on a 16-colour one in the 16. Each colour becomes the
  nearest the screen has.
- View > Dithering mixes dots of the nearest colours, for photos. Turn it
  off for drawings with flat colours.
- A big picture fills in from the bottom while it is read. One needs
  about a byte a pixel of memory (1024 x 768: 768 KB).
