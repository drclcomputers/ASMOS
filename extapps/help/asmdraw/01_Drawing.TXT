# Drawing

ASMDraw draws pictures in 16 colours, or 24 on a 256-colour screen
(Settings > Display).

- The tools, on the left: pencil, eraser, fill, pick a colour, line,
  spray, rectangle and filled rectangle, ellipse and filled ellipse.
  Under them, 6 brush sizes (1 to 9 pixels): pencil, eraser, spray and
  the lines of shapes use them.
- Shift while dragging: lines straight or at 45 degrees, squares,
  circles.
- Pick a colour: click the picture and the pen takes that colour
  (Shift+click: the paper), then it goes back to the tool you had.
- Click a colour for the pen, Shift+click one for the paper (the eraser
  draws with it). The two squares at the bottom left are the pen over
  the paper: click them to swap.
- With 256 colours, the last 8 in the strip are your own: white at
  first. Click one (it gets a frame), then Edit... (or double-click it):
  the colour picker. What is drawn in it changes as you choose. They are
  remembered, and saved in the picture.
- File: New, Open, Save, Save As, Print. Pictures are .PIC or .BMP
  files: Save As picks by the name's ending (UNTITLED.BMP). A .BMP is
  saved with 16 colours if only those are used, else 256; any .BMP
  opens, its colours made the nearest the screen has. A bigger one is
  cut to the window.
- On a 16-colour screen a picture made with more becomes the nearest
  of the 16.
