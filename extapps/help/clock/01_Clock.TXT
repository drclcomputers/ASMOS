# Clock

The time and the date, from the PC's clock (Settings > Clock sets them).

- View > Classic Clock shows a face with hands (the red one counts the
  seconds); View > Text Clock goes back to the numbers. Clock keeps the
  one you chose for the next time.
- Alarm > Set Alarm: - and + under Hour and Minutes change the time (hold
  one down to keep going), Ring at this time turns it on or off, then
  OK. Clear Alarm takes it away.
- The alarm rings even when Clock is closed.
