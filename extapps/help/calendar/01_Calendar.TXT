# Calendar

A month at a time, with a note for any day.

- Click a day to pick it. A grey day of the month before or after goes
  to that month.
- Double-click or Enter writes or changes the day's note; Del deletes it.
- The arrows move a day or a week, PgUp and PgDn a month, Home goes to
  today.
- View chooses whether the week starts on Monday or Sunday.

The notes are kept in /PREFS/CALNOTES.TXT.
